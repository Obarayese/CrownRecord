FAQ

Do I need to be the meeting host?
No. You record what appears in the meeting window on your computer. You do not need the host to enable Zoom/Meet cloud recording.
Does Crown Softech store my video?
No. Recordings are saved locally on your PC when you stop.
How is this different from Loom?
Loom focuses on instant cloud share links. CrownRecord is built for local download first — meetings, demos, and pitches without an upload step.
Can I use an external webcam and choose my microphone?
Yes. Pick devices in the Audio and Camera sections before recording (like Loom).
Will I see a preview while recording?
Yes. The app shows a live preview of the screen (and webcam bubble if enabled) while you record.
macOS or Linux?
Windows today. Mac and Linux are on the roadmap.
